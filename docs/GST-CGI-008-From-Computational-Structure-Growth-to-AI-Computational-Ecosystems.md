## Abstract

GST-CGI-007 defined **Computational Growth Intelligence (CGI)** as:

> **The capability of an intelligent system to construct, refine, validate, compose, delegate, promote, and govern the computational structures through which future intelligence is performed.**

This immediately raises a larger question.

What happens when computational growth is no longer confined to one AI runtime?

A structural runtime may generate a Leaf Sandbox Package. A specialized AI may consume that package, perform delegated structural unfolding, return evidence and candidate deltas, and thereby contribute to future structural growth. Multiple structural services may contribute different perspectives. Computational packages may be composed. Local structures may specialize. Policy may govern which capabilities cross system boundaries.

The result is no longer merely an intelligent program with a growing internal structure.

It begins to resemble an **AI Computational Ecosystem**.

In such an ecosystem, the primary exchange objects need not be limited to prompts, messages, documents, or tool calls.

They may include:

```text id="6zpr0h"
Structure

Evidence

Capability

Policy

Delta

Result
```

This article develops the transition:

```text id="jyx8zf"
Computational-Structure Growth
        ↓
Structural Packaging
        ↓
Delegated Structural Unfolding
        ↓
AI-to-AI Structural Exchange
        ↓
Structural Composition
        ↓
Distributed Evidence and Delta
        ↓
Governed Promotion
        ↓
Computational-System Growth
        ↓
AI Computational Ecosystem
```

The central proposition is:

> **An AI computational ecosystem emerges when intelligent systems can exchange bounded computational structures, evidence, capabilities, policies, results, and candidate deltas while preserving explicit authority boundaries and governed structural growth.**

This is more than conventional multi-agent coordination.

The systems do not merely communicate.

They can provide one another with localized computational structure.

The larger transition is therefore:

> **From Answer-as-a-Service, to Structural-Computation-as-a-Service, to a policy-governed ecosystem of growing computational intelligence.**

# 5. Structure


# 6. Evidence

**Evidence** answers:

It may include:

```text id="f84k2c"

counter-evidence

validation history

uncertainty

```

Evidence allows structural exchange to remain inspectable.

# 7. Capability

**Capability** answers:


Capability is explicitly bounded.

# 8. Policy


# 9. Delta


Delta is a proposal for possible growth.


Result serves the current task.

This distinction is fundamental.
