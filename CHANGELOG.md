# Changelog

All notable changes to **Generalized Structural Trigger and Computational Growth Intelligence (GST-CGI)** will be documented in this file.

The format follows a research-repository-oriented versioning model.

## [1.0.0] — 2026-09-15

### Initial Public Research Release

First canonical release of:

**Generalized Structural Trigger and Computational Growth Intelligence (GST-CGI)**

**From Two-Way CCC and Leaf Resolution to Delegated Unfolding and AI Computational-System Growth**

This release establishes a structural-intelligence framework connecting generalized CCC triggers, evidence-bearing and policy-governed structural nodes, leaf resolution, explicit Leftover, delegated structural computation, Delta Intelligence, computational-structure growth, and AI computational-system growth.

---

### Added — Generalized Structural Trigger

Introduced the **Generalized Structural Trigger (GST)** abstraction.

Canonical model:

```text
GST = <S, C, B, E, P>
```

where:

```text
S = Structural State
C = Comparison / Metric Semantics
B = Controlled Behavior
E = Evidence APIs
P = Policy / Perspective
```

Established the principle:

> **DNA is one encoding of a CCC trigger, not the definition of a CCC trigger.**

Expanded the trigger space from passive String DNA toward:

```text
Passive Trigger
    ↓
Structural Trigger
    ↓
Behavioral Trigger
    ↓
Generalized Structural Trigger
```

---

### Added — Evidence-Bearing and Policy-Governed Triggers

Introduced first-class support for:

* structural state;
* structural comparison;
* controlled behavior;
* supporting evidence;
* counter-evidence;
* applicability;
* uncertainty;
* provenance;
* runtime trace;
* policy-selected perspective.

Established the plugin/runtime boundary:

> **Plugin supplies domain intelligence; Runtime retains structural authority.**

---

### Added — Generalized Two-Way CCC

Extended Two-Way CCC beyond String-DNA-only trigger semantics.

Established the distinction:

```text
Metric Differential Tree
→ Structural Localization Space

Two-Way CCC
→ Structural Discrimination / Dispatch Space
```

Generalized CCC discrimination can now conceptually operate over trajectories, sequences, graphs, CallingGraphs, metric objects, policy objects, behavioral structures, and other sufficiently comparable structural objects.

---

### Added — Leaf Resolution Gate

Introduced the **Leaf Resolution Gate (LRG)**.

Established:

> **A structural leaf is not necessarily a decision leaf.**

Canonical leaf-resolution outcomes:

```text
DECIDE
REFINE
LEFTOVER
DELEGATE
```

Introduced **Decision Sufficiency** as the policy-relative criterion for determining whether current localization is sufficient for the requested decision.

Established:

> **Leafhood is structural; leaf resolution is policy-relative.**

---

### Added — Explicit Leftover

Promoted **LEFTOVER** from an implicit failure condition to a first-class structural runtime outcome.

LEFTOVER represents a legitimate boundary of current mature structure rather than a forced A/B classification.

Established:

> **Every governed structural discriminator should have explicit Leftover semantics.**

Distinguished:

```text
REFINE
=
Known need
+
Available mature differentiation path
```

from:

```text
LEFTOVER
=
Unresolved need
+
Insufficient mature structural support
```

---

### Added — Delta Intelligence

Introduced **Delta Intelligence** as the mechanism for converting unresolved structural experience into candidate computational growth.

Canonical loop:

```text
LEFTOVER
    ↓
Candidate Difference
    ↓
Evidence
    ↓
Counter-Evidence
    ↓
Candidate A/B CCC
    ↓
Validation
    ↓
Policy Gate
    ↓
Promotion
    ↓
New Mature Structure
```

Established:

> **Once Leftover becomes a first-class runtime outcome, Delta Intelligence becomes a first-class runtime obligation.**

---

### Added — Leaf Sandbox Package

Introduced the **Leaf Sandbox Package (LSP)**.

Canonical form:

```text
LSP = <C, T, E, B, P, K, R>
```

where:

```text
C = Structural Context
T = Generalized Structural Trigger
E = Evidence Bundle
B = Permitted Behavior
P = Policy Projection
K = Capability Boundary
R = Runtime Contract
```

An LSP converts a localized internal leaf into a policy-bounded computational package suitable for external computation.

Established the governing principle:

> **Export capability, not authority.**

---

### Added — Delegated Structural Unfolding

Introduced **Delegated Structural Unfolding (DSU)**.

Canonical path:

```text
Internal Leaf
    ↓
Policy Projection
    ↓
Leaf Sandbox Package
    ↓
External Intelligence
    ↓
Result
+
Evidence
+
Counter-Evidence
+
Candidate Delta
    ↓
Parent Validation
```

Established the distinction:

```text
Result
≠
Delta
```

A delegated system may produce both an immediate task result and a candidate reusable structural difference.

External systems do not automatically acquire structural promotion authority.

---

### Added — Structural Sovereignty

Introduced the principle of **Structural Sovereignty**.

External intelligence may:

```text
Compute
Discover
Simulate
Verify
Search Counter-Evidence
Propose Delta
```

while the governed parent runtime retains authority to:

```text
Validate
Reject
Promote
Rollback
Govern Mature Structure
```

This provides a foundation for bounded AI-to-AI structural computation.

---

### Added — Computational Growth Intelligence

Introduced **Computational Growth Intelligence (CGI)**.

Canonical definition:

> **Computational Growth Intelligence is the capability of an intelligent system to construct, refine, validate, delegate, compose, and govern the computational structures through which future intelligence is performed.**

Distinguished CGI from:

* ordinary learning;
* optimization;
* reasoning;
* memory accumulation;
* tool use;
* generic multi-agent orchestration.

Established:

> **Reasoning computes within structure; Computational Growth can change the structure within which future reasoning occurs.**

---

### Added — AI Growth Ladder

Introduced the four-level **AI Growth Ladder**:

```text
Level 1
Experience Growth
        ↓
Level 2
Performance / Score Growth
        ↓
Level 3
Computational-Structure Growth
        ↓
Level 4
Computational-System Growth
```

Established the corresponding conceptual progression:

* Experience Growth makes AI remember more.
* Performance Growth makes AI perform better.
* Computational-Structure Growth changes how AI computes.
* Computational-System Growth changes how intelligences organize computation together.

Introduced a separate **Growth Automation Axis**:

```text
Manual
    ↓
Human-Assisted
    ↓
Semi-Automatic
    ↓
Policy-Governed Automatic
    ↓
Policy-Governed Autonomous Growth
```

The growth-object axis and automation axis are explicitly treated as orthogonal.

---

### Added — Computational-System Growth

Introduced **Computational-System Growth** as growth in reusable computational relationships among intelligent systems.

Possible growth objects include:

```text
Certified Delegation Edge

AI Computational CallingGraph

Structural Exchange Protocol

Specialist AI Relationship

Composite Computational Path
```

This extends structural growth beyond the internal anatomy of one AI.

Canonical AI-to-AI exchange objects:

```text
DELTA
```

Established the progression:

```text
One-Time Delegation
```

```text

        ↓

        ↓
```
